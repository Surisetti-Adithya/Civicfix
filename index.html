Your toolkit

Three things that turn a complaint into a fix.

Complaint reporting

Choose a category, describe the problem and pin the exact spot. Your report reaches the right department with a clear location.

Community verification

Neighbours can confirm a problem is real. Reports with more confirmations are marked as verified and move up the queue.

Issue tracking

Follow each report from submitted to resolved. You'll see when it is assigned, when work starts and when it is closed.

How to use CivicFix

  1. Report

    Open the report page from the top of any screen. Pick a category, say where it is and describe the problem.

  2. Verify

    Go to Browse Issues and confirm problems you have seen yourself. More confirmations mean faster action.

  3. Track

    Use your reference number on Browse Issues to check where your report stands until it is resolved.
